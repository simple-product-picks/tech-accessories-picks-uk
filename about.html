About Simple Product Picks

An independent UK tech accessories review site.

What we do

Simple Product Picks is a UK-based review site focused on USB-C charging accessories: chargers, hubs, cables, power banks and adapters. We research and verify the best options available on Amazon UK so you don't have to spend hours comparing specs.

How we choose products

Every product we recommend meets a strict set of criteria:

  • Trusted brands only - we stick to a shortlist of proven names with UK support and honest spec sheets (Anker, UGREEN, Belkin, ESR, INIU and similar)
  • Every link verified - each recommendation is checked against the live Amazon UK listing, so the link lands on the exact product we describe
  • Real specs checked - wattage, port count, charging standards (GaN, PD, PPS) verified against manufacturer data
  • Scored on our own rubric - every pick earns an SPP Score out of 10 from our published criteria (see how we test), independent of anyone else's ratings
  • Value at each tier - we pick a best overall, a best budget, and a best premium so there's a recommendation for every budget

Who writes this site

Simple Product Picks is written and maintained by a UK-based tech enthusiast with years of hands-on experience across consumer tech, from PCs, phones and smart home kit to the charging and connectivity gear this site specialises in. Plenty of it has been bought, tested, returned and lived with along the way. That background matters here, because accessories are where the most confusing claims concentrate: chargers that claim 100W but only deliver it on one port, hubs that drop 4K to 30Hz, cables that quietly throttle charging speeds. Our goal is to cut through the noise.

How we stay up to date

We review and update our picks regularly. The Amazon UK catalogue changes constantly, so we re-verify our links on a rolling basis and refresh a guide whenever a better product earns its place. We deliberately do not display prices or star ratings; for current pricing, availability and reviews, check the product page on Amazon UK via any of our links.
