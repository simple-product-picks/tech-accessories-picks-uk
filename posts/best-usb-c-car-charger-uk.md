## FAQ

**Q: Can a car charger damage my phone?**
A: No. USB-C Power Delivery negotiates between the charger and the device, so the phone draws only what it asks for. The charger's rated wattage is the ceiling, not what it pushes into every connected device.

**Q: What fuse does my car's cigarette lighter use?**
A: It varies by make and model. Check your owner's manual or the label on the fuse box cover, usually under the dashboard or in the boot. Common ratings are 10A, 15A or 20A. At 12 volts, a 10A fuse gives roughly 120W before it blows, and a 15A fuse gives roughly 180W.

**Q: Can I charge a laptop from a car charger?**
A: Some laptops, yes. You need a charger that delivers enough watts for the laptop's minimum draw, and a USB-C cable rated to carry it. The UGREEN here lists PD140W, which covers most USB-C laptops. Check your laptop's rated input before relying on it.
