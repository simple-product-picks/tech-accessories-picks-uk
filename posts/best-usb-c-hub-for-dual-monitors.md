## FAQ

**Q: Can any USB-C hub drive two monitors at once?**
A: No. Two-display output depends on both the hub's HDMI/USB-C outputs and your laptop's USB-C video capabilities, so it's worth confirming your machine supports it before you buy.

**Q: What does the PD wattage mean for my laptop?**
A: Power Delivery wattage is how much charge the hub can pass through to your laptop while you work; a higher figure like 100W suits more power-hungry laptops, while 85W is fine for many mainstream models.

**Q: Do I need a docking station or is a compact hub enough?**
A: If you want lots of ports at a fixed desk, a docking station suits you; if you mainly need dual video and charging in a portable shape, a compact dual-HDMI hub is plenty.
