How we make money and how it affects our recommendations.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. This means if you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from Amazon at no extra cost to you.

We are a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.co.uk.

Editorial independence

Our recommendations are based on durable specifications (wattage, port selection, charging standards supported), brand track record, verification of every link against the live Amazon UK listing, and value for money. We do not display prices or star ratings; check the product page on Amazon UK for current details. Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we recommend — we would not recommend a product we do not believe is a good pick for UK buyers.

Pricing and availability

Prices and availability shown on this site were verified at the time of publication. Amazon prices fluctuate — always check the current price on Amazon UK before purchasing. We aim to update pages regularly but cannot guarantee real-time accuracy.
