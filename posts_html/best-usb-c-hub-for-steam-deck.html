FAQ

Q: Will a USB-C hub charge my Steam Deck while it is connected to the TV? A: Yes, as long as the hub has a USB-C power delivery input and you plug the Deck's charger into it. All three picks here support pass-through charging, so the Deck stays powered while docked.

Q: Do I need Ethernet, or is Wi-Fi enough? A: Wi-Fi is fine for play, but a wired Ethernet connection gives more consistent download speeds and lower latency online, which is why the Revodok hubs include a Gigabit port.

Q: Can I connect a keyboard, mouse and controller through one hub? A: Yes. The USB-A ports on these hubs handle keyboards, mice and wired or dongle-based controllers, so you can build a full desktop-style setup from the Deck's single port.
