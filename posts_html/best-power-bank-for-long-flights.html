FAQ

Q: Can I take a power bank on a plane in my hand luggage? A: Spare lithium batteries and power banks must travel in your cabin baggage, never in checked luggage, and most airlines cap capacity around the 100Wh mark (roughly 27000mAh) without prior approval. Always check your specific airline's watt-hour limits before you fly.

Q: What capacity power bank do I need for a long flight? A: A 20000mAh pack will recharge most phones several times over a long-haul journey, while a larger 25000mAh pack makes sense if you are charging tablets or a laptop too.

Q: Can a power bank charge a laptop on a flight? A: Only higher-wattage banks with enough output, such as the 87W and 200W models here, can charge most laptops; lower-output packs are better suited to phones and tablets.
