FAQ

Q: Can a power bank charge a laptop? A: Yes, as long as it has a USB-C Power Delivery port with enough wattage. Most ultrabooks charge happily from a 100W port, while a 16-inch MacBook Pro can draw up to 140W, so match the pack's output to your machine.

Q: What wattage power bank do I need for a MacBook Pro? A: A 14-inch MacBook Pro tops out around 96W, so a 100W pack covers it. The 16-inch model ships with a 140W adapter, so you need a pack with a 140W USB-C output to charge it at full speed.

Q: Do I need a special cable for 140W charging? A: Yes. Charging above 100W uses the USB-C Extended Power Range, which needs a cable rated to 240W (5A). A standard USB-C cable still works but caps the charge at 100W, which is why the packs here with a built-in or bundled 140W cable are handy.
