FAQ

Q: Do I need an adapter that supports 4K at 60Hz? A: If you mainly browse, work in documents or watch video, 4K at 60Hz gives smoother motion and a more comfortable feel. Basic 4K adapters often run at 30Hz, so check the spec if smoothness matters to you.

Q: Will a USB-C to HDMI adapter work with my phone or tablet? A: It depends on whether your device's USB-C port supports video output (often called DisplayPort Alt Mode), so check your device's specifications, as not every USB-C port can drive an external display.

Q: Should I choose a single adapter or a multi-port hub? A: A single adapter is smaller and ideal if you only need HDMI, while a hub is better if you also want Ethernet, extra USB ports or to connect several accessories from one port.
