FAQ

Q: What is the best USB hub for a laptop? A: For most laptops a seven-port hub lands in the right place, which is why the UGREEN Revodok 7-in-1 is our overall pick: it adds a screen, wired Ethernet, USB-A and a card reader without the size of a full dock. Step down to a 5-in-1 if you only need a monitor and a couple of ports, or up to a 10-in-1 dock if you run two screens.

Q: Are USB-C hubs any good, or do they cause problems? A: A decent one from an established brand is reliable for everyday display, charging and data. The catch is usually the laptop rather than the hub: a USB-C port has to support DisplayPort Alt Mode before any hub can push video out of it, and some budget laptops and tablets ship data-only ports. Check that first, then match the hub to what you'll actually ask of it.

Q: Does it matter which brand of USB hub I buy? A: It does for support and consistency. We stick to established names like Anker and UGREEN because their listings are clear about wattage and display support, and because a brand still trading in two years is one that can still honour a warranty. All three picks here come from those brands.
