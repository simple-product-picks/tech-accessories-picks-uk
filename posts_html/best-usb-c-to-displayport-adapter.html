FAQ

Q: Will any USB-C port work with these adapters? A: No - your laptop, tablet, or phone needs a USB-C port that supports DisplayPort Alt Mode. Check your device's specifications before buying, as not every USB-C port carries a video signal.

Q: What is the difference between DisplayPort 1.4 and DisplayPort 2.1? A: DisplayPort 2.1 carries far more bandwidth (up to 80Gbps on the premium pick here), allowing higher resolutions and refresh rates than DisplayPort 1.4. For standard 4K, 1.4 is usually plenty; for the newest high-end monitors, 2.1 gives more headroom.

Q: Can I use one of these to game at a high refresh rate? A: Yes, provided your device, the adapter, and your monitor all support the resolution and refresh rate you want. DisplayPort 1.4 handles high-refresh 4K, while DisplayPort 2.1 goes further for the most demanding displays.
