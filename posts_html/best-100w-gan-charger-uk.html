FAQ

Q: What does GaN mean and why does it matter? A: GaN (gallium nitride) lets chargers run cooler and smaller than older silicon designs, so a 100W brick stays compact enough to carry every day without sacrificing output.

Q: Can a 100W GaN charger charge my laptop? A: Most USB-C laptops charge fine from a 100W single port, but check your laptop's required wattage first, as a few high-performance machines ask for more than 100W.

Q: Do I need a special cable to get the full 100W? A: Yes, you need a USB-C to USB-C cable rated for 100W (5A) with the right standards; a thin or low-rated cable will cap your charging speed regardless of the charger.
