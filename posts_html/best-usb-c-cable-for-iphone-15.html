FAQ

Q: Does the iPhone 15 need a USB-C cable? A: Yes, the iPhone 15 range uses a USB-C port rather than Lightning, so a USB-C to USB-C cable is the standard way to charge and sync it.

Q: Will a 100W cable damage my iPhone? A: No, a higher wattage rating is just a ceiling. Your iPhone only draws the power it is designed to take, so the extra headroom simply means the cable is not the limiting factor.

Q: Why pick a braided cable over a plain one? A: Braided cables tend to resist fraying, kinking, and everyday wear better than plain rubberised cables, which often helps them last longer in a bag or by the bedside.
