FAQ

Q: Do I need drivers to use a USB-C Ethernet adapter? A: Many modern adapters, including the Amazon Basics pick, are plug and play on current versions of Windows and macOS, though some chipsets may prompt a quick driver download the first time.

Q: Is Gigabit or 2.5G Ethernet better for me? A: For standard UK home broadband, Gigabit is plenty and you won't see a difference; 2.5G only pays off if your broadband and the rest of your network actually support multi-gigabit speeds.

Q: Will a USB-C Ethernet adapter work with my tablet or phone? A: It depends on the device, as many USB-C tablets and some phones support wired Ethernet through an adapter, but you should confirm your specific model supports USB-C networking before buying.
