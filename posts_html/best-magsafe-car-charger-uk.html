FAQ

Q: Will a MagSafe car charger work with my iPhone? A: MagSafe magnetic charging works with iPhone 12 and later; older iPhones can still rest on the magnet but won't get the full MagSafe alignment and may need a compatible case or adapter.

Q: Do I need a separate adapter to power a MagSafe car charger? A: Most wireless car chargers draw power via a USB car adapter and cable, so check what's included and make sure your car socket and adapter can supply enough power; see the product page on Amazon UK for details.

Q: Is wireless charging fast enough for a car journey? A: For top-ups during a typical drive it's usually fine, though wired charging is generally quicker if you need to recharge rapidly on a short trip.
